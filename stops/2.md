Lorna Selim was an important member of the Baghdad Modern Art Group. Born in England, she moved to Baghdad after marrying Iraqi artist Jewad Selim in 1950. She became closely connected to the artistic community developing in Baghdad during the 1950s and contributed to the group's exploration of modern Iraqi art. 

Selim developed her own artistic language while working within the cultural environment of Baghdad. Her work often focused on everyday life, Iraqi women, traditional clothing, and the architecture of the city. She combined these familiar subjects with simplified shapes and modern artistic approaches, creating a connection between everyday Iraqi life and modernism.
