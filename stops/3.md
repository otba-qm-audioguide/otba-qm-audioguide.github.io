Khalifa's Wife, created in 1968, is part of this early period of Azzawi's artistic career. The work is an oil painting on canvas measuring 99 by 85 centimetres  


Dia al Azzawi was born in Baghdad in 1939. He studied archaeology at the University of Baghdad, graduating in 1962, and later completed evening studies at the Institute of Fine Arts in Baghdad in 1964. During his studies, he became involved in Baghdad's artistic community and attended the Free Atelier run by artist Hafidh al-Droubi. 

Although Azzawi joined the Impressionist Group formed by Hafidh al-Droubi, the work he was doing was closer to that of the Baghdad Group for Modern Art
