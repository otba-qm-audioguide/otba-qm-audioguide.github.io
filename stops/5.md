This **Baghdadiat**, created in 1956, is a mixed-media work on hardboard measuring 98.5 by 169 centimetres.   

A central idea in Selim's artistic thinking was drawing inspiration from tradition. His approach is a relationship between the past and the present, in which artists could engage with heritage while developing new contemporary aesthetics.

Selim gave the name **Baghdadiat** to a group of paintings from the 1950s. The name refers to the Abbasid Baghdad School of painting and to the contemporary artistic revitalisation that Selim perceived in Baghdad at the time. They consisted of three groups of paintings: one having its source in folk life and traditions, the second in vernacular artifacts, and the third in the innocence and joy of children.
