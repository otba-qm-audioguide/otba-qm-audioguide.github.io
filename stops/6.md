This final stop looks at the legacy of the Baghdad Modern Art Group and how its ideas continued beyond the group's original members and active years.

The group was established in 1951 by **Jewad Selim** and **Shakir Hassan Al Said**. Its influence extended to later generations of Iraqi artists, including through education and succeeding artistic groups. Mathaf notes that the group's ideas continued to be disseminated through artists of later generations, including the so-called Eighties Generation.

One example is the sculptor **Mohammed Ghani Hikmat**, who was an influential member of the Baghdad Group of Modern Art. His work drew inspiration from Iraq's heritage, while his public sculptures became important markers of memory and Iraqi cultural identity.
