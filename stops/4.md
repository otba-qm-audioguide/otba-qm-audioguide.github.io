This work, Title Unknown, was created in 1961. It is an oil painting on canvas measuring 82 by 94 centimetres 

Shakir Hassan Al Said was born in Samawah, Iraq, in 1925 and died in Baghdad in 2004. He was one of the two artists who established the Baghdad Modern Art Group in 1951, together with Jewad Selim. The group sought to develop modern art in Baghdad while engaging with the city's history and the artistic heritage of Iraq.  

Al Said's artistic practice developed significantly during the 1950s and 1970s. During this period, he explored new forms of representation, moving from landscape towards geometry and from the human body towards form. 

His work was also connected to his ideas about the physical nature of painting. His use of textures and surfaces, including traces and scratched surfaces resembling walls and manuscripts
